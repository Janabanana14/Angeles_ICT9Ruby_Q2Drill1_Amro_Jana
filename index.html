🏐 Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Learn the basics, enjoy the game, and have fun!

Basic Rules

  • Each team has six players on the court.
  • Serve the ball over the net.
  • A team can touch the ball up to three times.
  • Do not catch or hold the ball.
  • A set is usually played to 25 points.

Volleyball Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Net and court
  • Knee pads
  • Sports shoes
  • Comfortable sports uniform

Basic Techniques

  • Serving: Start the rally.
  • Passing: Receive the ball.
  • Setting: Prepare the ball for a teammate.
  • Spiking: Hit the ball toward the opponent's court.
  • Blocking: Stop an attack at the net.
